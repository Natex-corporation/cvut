Do these first

Activate your university identity

One identity problem can make KOS, email, Moodle, Wi-Fi, and faculty services all appear broken. Resolve this phase before troubleshooting each service separately.

Use the university’s activation instructions and sign in only on an official CTU page. A password manager is safer than reusing a personal password.

Know where work happens

Open the core study systems

KOS is the official academic record; Moodle is the course workspace. They can show different information without either system being broken.

Find your programme, enrolled courses, timetable records, and exam area. Treat KOS as the authority for enrollment, classifications, and the official study plan.

When a checkbox is blocked

Diagnose the smallest failing layer

Several services reject your login
Return to the CTU password and UserMap first. One identity, role, or activation issue can affect many destinations together.
Only eduroam fails
Use the Wi-Fi guide and verify the network-specific username, password, and certificate setup. Do not repeatedly change your main password without checking the instructions.
KOS works but a Moodle course is missing
Confirm enrollment in KOS, allow time for synchronization, then check the teacher’s or faculty’s course instructions before opening a technical ticket.
You do not know who owns the problem
Use the support decision guide. Share the service name and a non-sensitive description, never a password, recovery code, or private study record.
