01

KOS or Moodle?

KOS holds enrollment, exams, grades, and official study records. Moodle holds materials, assignments, quizzes, and course announcements.

02

CTU password or eduroam?

Your main CTU password opens most university systems. Eduroam uses separate network credentials, so fix it through the Wi-Fi instructions.

03

Student Card or Card Portal?

The Student Card page explains eligibility and collection. The Card Portal is the self-service place for photos, card administration, and digital ISIC services.

04

ServiceDesk or CIPS?

Use ServiceDesk for a broken university service or account. Use CIPS for academic, psychological, social-legal, or personal support.
