About

CVUT Crossroad is an unofficial shortcut page for students who need quick access to CVUT study systems, Moodle, account tools, Wi-Fi guides, libraries, cards, and campus services.

Scope

The current version focuses only on CVUT because the domain is cvut-crossroad.com. The goal is practical navigation, not replacing official CVUT websites.

What This Site Adds

Many official services use different domains, Czech and English page variants, and faculty-specific instructions. This site keeps those entry points together, explains the normal student task behind each link, and lets visitors save useful shortcuts locally on their own device.

Editorial Rules

  • Prefer official university pages and official service domains.
  • Keep descriptions short and useful for students.
  • Remove or update links when official pages move.
  • Cover student wellbeing, international support, careers, and community as well as core study systems.
  • Do not collect school passwords or personal study data.

Independence

This site is not operated by CVUT or Google. University names are used only to identify linked public services.

What This Site Does Not Do

CVUT Crossroad does not proxy logins, copy private university systems, collect study records, or publish deadlines as if they were official. When a linked service has its own rules, the official page should be treated as the final authority.
