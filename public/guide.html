New student sequence

Your first 30 minutes

Do these in order. Later services depend on your CTU identity being active, so solving the account first prevents several confusing failures at once.

Want to track this privately as you go? Use the interactive first-year checklist; progress stays in this browser.

  1. Set the initial CTU password. Follow the official initial-password instructions. Never enter the password on this site.
  2. Check UserMap. Confirm your identity, faculty role, contacts, and account status in UserMap.
  3. Prepare the student card. Read the card requirements, then use the Card Portal for photo and self-service tasks.
  4. Open KOS and Outlook. KOS is your official study record; Outlook is where important university communication arrives.
  5. Find your Moodle courses. Moodle is the teaching workspace. A course can exist in KOS before it appears in Moodle.
  6. Configure eduroam. Use the official setup guide because Wi-Fi credentials are not always identical to the main CTU login.

Troubleshooting

When a login or course is missing

Several CVUT systems reject the login
Start with the CTU password and UserMap. A central identity or role problem can affect several services together.
Only eduroam fails
Use the eduroam password and setup instructions. Changing the main CTU password does not necessarily repair Wi-Fi credentials.
A course is in KOS but not Moodle
Check that enrollment is complete, allow time for synchronization, then follow the course or faculty instructions. Ask the teacher before opening a technical ticket when enrollment looks correct.
Your role or faculty access is missing
Confirm the role in UserMap. If the official record is wrong or does not propagate, send a ServiceDesk request with the service name and a non-sensitive description.
Keep support requests safe.

Never send passwords, recovery codes, full identity documents, or screenshots containing private study records. Support staff can ask for safer identifiers if needed.

People who can help

Technical, academic, and personal support

Not every problem is an IT ticket. Choosing the right support route usually gets a faster and more useful answer.

  • ServiceDesk: unavailable systems, access problems, incidents, and official service requests.
  • CIPS: free academic, psychological, social-legal, coaching, personal-development, and crisis support.
  • ELSA: accessibility services and study adaptations for students with specific needs.
  • Your faculty: study-department questions, local rules, programme requirements, rooms, labs, and faculty-specific services.

Beyond coursework

Research, careers, and community

The CTU Library offers licensed journals, e-books, standards, citation databases, and research support. The Career Centre provides counselling, workshops, mentoring, and employer connections. The Student Union and university events calendar are the quickest routes into clubs, talks, sport, culture, and campus life.

These opportunities are easy to miss because they live on different websites, so save the useful ones on the main directory. Saved links stay only in your browser.
